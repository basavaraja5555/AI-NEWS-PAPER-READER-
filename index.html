How to import newspapers and Telegram exports
  1. Click Choose files. In the file picker, open Downloads, select PDFs, PPTX files, or supported exports, and click Open. Optionally enter a PDF page range such as 1-5,8; leave it blank to import all pages.
  2. Selectable PDF text is extracted page by page. PPTX slide text is extracted locally, with each readable slide added as a study story. Images embedded in slides are not OCR-read; older .ppt files should be saved as .pptx first.
  3. Scanned PDFs are rendered temporarily in your browser for OCR. Low-confidence text, common page headers and footers, and obvious ad blocks are filtered; the remaining text is grouped into best-effort article cards. Only recognised text is saved, not page images; pages are saved as they finish. Reimporting replaces only the selected pages from that PDF and preserves the others. Some ads or scan errors may remain.
  4. The OCR engine and language models download from public CDNs the first time; the newspaper PDF itself stays on your device. First-time OCR can be slow.
  5. For Telegram newspaper posts: in Telegram Desktop open Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data, export selected chats as JSON or HTML, then import those files here.

This page cannot scan Downloads by itself. Choose only files you want to import. Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Online headlines use free public Bing News RSS and FreeJobAlert RSS feeds through the local server; opening a headline takes you to its publisher. Start start-local.ps1 to enable online updates and open http://127.0.0.1:8765/. PDF.js, OCR files, and the PPTX zip parser load from public CDNs.
