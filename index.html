How to import newspapers and Telegram exports
  1. Click Choose newspaper files. In the file picker, open Downloads, select one or more PDFs, and click Open.
  2. Selectable PDF text is extracted in your browser, then divided into page-based study entries. Kannada and English scripts are both recognised.
  3. Scanned PDFs are rendered temporarily in your browser for OCR. Only recognised words are saved; page images are not saved or uploaded. Obvious advertisement text is filtered, but some ads may still appear and some article text near ads may be missed.
  4. The OCR engine and language models download from public CDNs the first time; the newspaper PDF itself stays on your device. First-time OCR can be slow.
  5. For Telegram newspaper posts: in Telegram Desktop open Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data, export selected chats as JSON or HTML, then import those files here.

This page cannot scan Downloads by itself. Choose only files you want to import. It doesn't connect to Telegram, fetch live newspapers, or upload newspaper contents. PDF.js and OCR library/model files load from public CDNs; the newspaper PDF stays on your device.
